## How this was measured

- **Invoices:** the 6 scenario PDFs and the 3 valid test inputs (missing_invoice_number, unknown_po, prompt_injection), run once each through `run_pipeline`. The upload rejections need no LLM and are covered by the automated tests.
- **Labels:** taken from the generators' data and frozen in `eval/ground_truth.json` before this run (sha256 `b39564b6af8ab0729279ef01e073e3065aa75e7e2590bd5e79437246294de878`). No rule, threshold or prompt was changed for this evaluation.
- **Text fields** (GSTIN, invoice number, PO reference): exact match. Where the document prints no value, only null is correct.
- **Amounts and dates:** exact value. Tax is correct only if every printed tax line is read correctly.
- **Bank account:** the full numbers are compared in memory; only match / no match is reported. Masked values would not be enough, because two accounts can share their last four digits. IFSC, vendor name and currency are not scored here.
- **Line items:** correct only if every product line and charge is right in all its cells. Descriptions ignore case and spacing.
- **Decision correct:** the outcome matches (and the risk level, where the scenario states one), and no stage before the decision failed.
- **Triggering check correct:** the expected check fired and nothing unexplained fired beside it. "Not evaluated" warnings are allowed because they follow from the expected failure, and the scan may also show low-confidence warnings.
- **Not scored:** the AI-written summary and drafted message, including on prompt_injection, where the scores show only that the extracted fields and the decision were unaffected.
- **Isolation:** a temporary database, reset before each invoice and deleted afterwards. The default database and the deployed application were not touched.
